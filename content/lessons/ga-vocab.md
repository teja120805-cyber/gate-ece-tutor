# Vocabulary: Words, Idioms and Phrases

> Synonyms, antonyms, fill-in-the-blank and idiom meanings appear as quick 1-mark GA questions.

## Intuition

You don't need a huge dictionary — you need strategies: use roots and affixes, read the context clues around the blank, and eliminate options that don't fit the tone or grammar of the sentence.

## Strategies

1. **Context clues:** signal words show the relationship between parts of a sentence.
   - *However, although, but* signal contrast.
   - *Because, therefore, so* signal cause and effect.
   - *Moreover, and* signal similarity.
2. **Roots and affixes:**
   - *bene-* (good), *mal-* (bad), *chron-* (time), *-phobia* (fear);
   - *ir-/in-/un-* (not), *re-* (again), *omni-* (all).
3. **Elimination:** reject options that are the wrong part of speech, or whose connotation (positive/negative) does not fit.

## Useful words and idioms

| Word | Meaning | Opposite |
|---|---|---|
| ephemeral | short-lived | permanent |
| meticulous | very careful and precise | careless |
| scarce | in short supply | abundant |
| ambiguous | open to more than one interpretation | clear |
| pragmatic | practical | idealistic |
| irrevocable | cannot be changed or undone | reversible |

| Idiom | Meaning |
|---|---|
| beat around the bush | avoid the main point |
| once in a blue moon | very rarely |
| a blessing in disguise | something good that seemed bad at first |
| hit the nail on the head | describe something exactly right |
| bite the bullet | face something difficult bravely |

## Worked examples

**Example 1.** "The committee's decision was ___; no one could change it." The clue "no one could change it" points to **irrevocable**.

**Example 2.** The antonym of *scarce* is *abundant*.

## Common traps

- Choosing a word with the right meaning but the wrong part of speech.
- Ignoring contrast signals such as *although*.

## Summary

- Read the context clues and signal words; use roots and affixes; eliminate by grammar and tone.
