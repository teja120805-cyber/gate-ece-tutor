# Mensuration and Geometry

> Areas, perimeters and volumes of standard shapes, plus basic triangle and circle properties.

## Key formulas

**2D shapes**

| Shape | Area | Perimeter |
|---|---|---|
| rectangle $l\times b$ | $lb$ | $2(l + b)$ |
| square side $a$ | $a^2$ | $4a$ |
| triangle | $\frac12bh$; Heron: $\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$ | $a + b + c$ |
| equilateral triangle | $\frac{\sqrt3}{4}a^2$ | $3a$ |
| circle radius $r$ | $\pi r^2$ | $2\pi r$ |
| sector (angle θ) | $\frac{\theta}{360°}\pi r^2$ | arc length $\frac{\theta}{360°}2\pi r$ |
| trapezium | $\frac12(a + b)h$ | — |

**3D solids**

| Solid | Volume | Surface area |
|---|---|---|
| cube side $a$ | $a^3$ | $6a^2$ |
| cuboid | $lbh$ | $2(lb + bh + hl)$ |
| cylinder | $\pi r^2h$ | curved $2\pi rh$, total $2\pi r(r + h)$ |
| cone | $\frac13\pi r^2h$ | curved $\pi rl$, with slant height $l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2}$ |
| sphere | $\frac43\pi r^3$ | $4\pi r^2$ |
| hemisphere | $\frac23\pi r^3$ | total $3\pi r^2$ |

## Geometry facts

- Pythagoras: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$; triples (3, 4, 5), (5, 12, 13), (8, 15, 17).
- Angle sum of a triangle is 180°; an $n$-gon's interior angles sum to $(n - 2)180°$.
- An angle in a semicircle is 90°; equal chords subtend equal angles.
- Similar figures: lengths scale by $k$, areas by $k^2$, volumes by $k^3$.

## Worked examples

**Example 1.** A circle with $r = 7$ ($\pi = 22/7$): area $= 154$.

**Example 2.** Legs 6 and 8: hypotenuse 10.

**Example 3.** A cube with surface area 54: $6a^2 = 54$ ⇒ $a = 3$ ⇒ volume 27.

**Example 4.** A rectangle with perimeter 30 and length 10: breadth 5, area 50.

**Example 5.** A cylinder with $r = 3$, $h = 7$: $V = \frac{22}{7}\times9\times7 = 198$.

## Summary

- Memorise the area and volume tables; use Pythagorean triples.
- Scaling: area ∝ $k^2$, volume ∝ $k^3$.
